# Privacy Policy for FitCheck

**Last updated: 2026-05-01**

## Overview
FitCheck ("the App") is an AI-powered outfit rating application. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Collect
- **Images**: Photos you upload or capture are sent to Google Gemini API for analysis. Images are not stored on our servers.
- **Local Storage**: Your outfit rating history is stored locally on your device only.
- **Camera Access**: Only used when you choose to take a photo. We do not record or store camera feeds.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect personal information
- We do not create user accounts
- We do not track usage analytics
- We do not share data with third parties (except Google Gemini API for image analysis)

## Google Gemini API
Images are processed by Google's Gemini API. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google handles this data.

## Children's Privacy
This app is not directed at children under 13.
