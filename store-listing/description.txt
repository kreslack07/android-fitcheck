FitCheck - AI Outfit Rater

Rate your outfits instantly with the power of Google Gemini AI. Upload a photo of your outfit and get professional fashion feedback in seconds.

FEATURES:
• AI-powered outfit analysis using Gemini Vision
• Score your outfit across multiple dimensions: style, color coordination, fit & silhouette, and versatility
• Get personalized suggestions to elevate your look
• Rate for different occasions: General, Casual, Formal, Date Night, Party
• Camera integration - snap a photo directly in the app
• Outfit history - track your last 10 rated outfits
• Works offline as a Progressive Web App

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Choose your occasion (Casual, Formal, Date, Party, or General)
2. Upload a photo or take one with your camera
3. Get instant AI feedback with scores and suggestions
4. Save to history and track your style evolution

Perfect for fashion enthusiasts, style beginners, or anyone who wants a second opinion before heading out the door.

Powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash Vision AI.
